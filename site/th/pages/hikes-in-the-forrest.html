เดินป่า


We organize guided hikes through the forest for our guests and visitors.

You may like to bring your group just for the day or for the weekend. Families with children are most welcome.

If you wish to join us for breakfast and lunch, please make arrangements with our office.

Activity Details
  • Hikes 3 hours along the forest paths and down by the stream
  • Leave after breakfast 9am (breakfast at 8am)
  • Back for lunch (lunch 12pm)
  • 10 people minimum per hike
  • Bring good shoes and swimming wear
  • If you come for breakfast and lunch please let our office know in advance
  • Price for the long Hike to the Rock Pool is 200 Baht per person
