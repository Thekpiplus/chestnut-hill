From the airport

About 25 km, a little over half an hour. We can send a car if you share your flight number and landing time.

From Hat Yai

Songthaew, taxi, or a joint transfer. The resort is also about 35 km from the Malaysian border.

From Penang

Drive north across the border. The roads are straightforward; the last stretch climbs into the rubber hills.

What to bring

Most everyday things are easy to buy in Hat Yai. For a first trip into this climate, pack light clothes, sandals, swimwear, a flashlight, and a plug adapter.

Also useful: mosquito repellent, bite balm, sunscreen, personal medicine, and a notebook if you are here to write.

Climate on the hill

  • Late December to February: cool and windy
  • March to April: hot and dry
  • May: some rain
  • July to mid-December: monsoon, hottest and wettest from October into early December

The air up here is cooler than in the city. The stream runs all year; the waterfall an hour north is fullest in the rains.
