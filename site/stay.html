Rooms

Cottages on the hillside.

Wooden houses, twin rooms, and a larger dormitory under the activity hall. Breakfast, housekeeping, water, and electricity are included.

Nightly rates move with the season. Ask us for the current price for your dates. Long stays are possible when a room is free.

Upper hillside

Ban Tonsala

A wooden cottage just below the sunrise viewpoint. One single bed, a private shower, and a large balcony for walking or yoga. Windows face west toward the mountains.

Upper hillside

Ban Tonsai

A wooden cottage with a wide deck for reading. Two single beds share the room, with a private shower and bathroom.

Lower hillside

Ban Shawmuwang

An elevated wooden cottage on pillars. Upper rooms are single bedrooms, each with a shower and a small balcony. Hammocks and a reading corner sit on the ground floor.

Best outlook

Ban Nakbud

Two neighbouring twin rooms on the western forest slope. Sandstone walls, slate floors, high ceilings, and insect-screened windows. The beds face the valley, the sunset, and a night sky full of stars.

Main lodge

Ban Romgaw

The largest lodging: five twin rooms and two small dormitories of six beds each. Mountain-facing rooms on the ground and middle floors, with a shared balcony between the dorms.

Groups

Tonsom Dormitory

A large ground-floor room beneath the activity hall, with up to 19 single beds. Four showers and toilets sit just outside. Windows face west to the mountains.

In every stay

  • Hot shower and a safety light in each room
  • Balcony, terrace, or a window onto the hill
  • Wi-Fi in the common areas
  • Organic meals from the resort garden
  • Cliff Terrace Kitchen, Lae Pa Cafe, and Niramaya Spa
  • Bicycles for getting around the grounds

House notes

  • Check-in from 14:00. Check-out by 12:00.
  • One child under 5 stays free in an existing bed.
  • An extra bed is 600 baht per person per night, with breakfast.
  • Individual bookings can be cancelled up to 7 days before arrival. Group bookings inside 30 days are non-refundable.
  • No alcohol on the property. Smoking is only at the entrance. Pets are not allowed.
  • Airport transfers fit 3 adults; ask for a van for larger groups and send your flight number.
