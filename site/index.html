Hat Yai · Songkhla · Thailand

A forest stay above a mountain stream.

Chestnut Hill Eco Resort is a quiet hillside of wooden cottages, home-grown food, and rock pools. About 25 km from Hat Yai, and still very much in the trees.

Plan a stay See the cottages
25 km from Hat Yai city and the airport, about half an hour by car.
Organic kitchen Vegetables and fruit from the resort garden, cooked on site.
Open all year Cooler mountain air, a stream that runs through every season.

The place

Away from the city, easy to reach.

The resort sits above a mountain stream, with low ranges on either side and rubber gardens in the hills around Hat Yai. Guests come to write, rest, hike with their children, or bring a small group for a workshop.

Rooms are simple and clean. Breakfast is included. Lunch and dinner are served at Cliff Terrace Kitchen, and coffee is poured at Lae Pa Cafe on the cliff.

Reasons to stay

Forest rooms, a working garden, and a day that can be as quiet or as full as you like.

Cottages with a view

Hillside wooden houses and twin rooms, each with a hot shower. Every room looks out on the mountain or the trees.

Food grown here

Banana, coconut, papaya, tomato, and herbs from the garden. Thai and international plates, with vegetarian cooking at the centre.

Wander

Stream and forest

Self-guided walks, rock pools for swimming, and a longer hike to the waterfall when you want a guide.
