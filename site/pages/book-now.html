​Our Room Types

Ban Tonsala: A wooden cottage on the upper hillside just below the Sunrise Viewpoint. Window facing west toward the mountains. 1 single bed. Shower and bathroom. Large balcony to walk and practice yoga. 

Ban Tonsai: A wooden cottage on the upper hillside. Balcony (wide deck) for reading or relaxing. Two single beds in one room with shower and bathroom. 
 
Ban Shawmuwang: A Lower hillside, elevated wooden cottage on pillars. On upper level, Rooms #1 and #2 – Single bed rooms. Shower and bathroom for each room. Small balcony. Hammock and reading area on ground floor. 

Ban Romgaw: This is the largest lodging. There are 5 twin rooms and 2 small dormitories. 
Room #7: Ground floor, with two large windows facing the mountain. Two single beds. One toilet and shower.
Rooms #1 and #4: Middle level. Each room has large windows with a mountain view. Two single beds per room. One toilet and shower per room.
Rooms #2 and #3: Middle level: Large windows with no mountain view. Two single beds per room. One toilet and shower per room. 
Rooms #5 and #6: Dormitory-style. 6 single beds per room. 2 toilets and showers per room. Windows facing east with the mountain. Large balcony interconnecting both rooms. 

Ban Nakbud: Perched on the western forest hillslope, with probably the best outlook of the eco resort, Nakbud offers 2 adjacent twin bedded rooms that benefit from added comfort and tasteful artisan designed interiors. 

Both rooms are decorated with traditional sandstone tiled walls, slate tiled floors and stay cool and pleasant with high ceilings and large slide-screen, insect-proof windows.The rooms have no balcony but look out onto vast skies and endless forests and valleys. The twin beds face the large windows, showcasing the red-tinted sunsets and star studded night skies. Guests can delight in falling asleep to the unique sounds of the tropical nature and waking to the forest birdsong.

Each room has its own en suite slate-tiled attractive artisanal bathroom with hot and cold showers, toilet and all essential amenities.

Tonsom Dormitory: This is a large room with single beds. Maximum capacity is 19 beds. Four showers with toilets are outside, just next to the dormitory. The windows face west, to the mountains. The dormitory is on the ground floor beneath the Activity Hall.
