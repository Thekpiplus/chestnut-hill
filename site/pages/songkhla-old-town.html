Songkhla Old Town

​The historic town of Songkhla (Songkhla Old Town) is worth visiting for its charming traditional architectural style with plenty of museums, old Shoppe houses, and delicious traditional local food only found in Songkhla. The old town consist fo three major roads: Nakorn Noak Road, Nakorn Nai Road, and lastly Nang Ngam Road.

​Tourists can visit many historical places such as the old rice mill Hub Ho Hin and Sino-Portuguese old houses. Now many signages are in English so tourist can understand more about this interest old town. 

Below are some of the common sights you will see if you visit Songkhla

  • Baan Nakorn Nai Museum 
  • Baan Nai Nakhon  Butique Hotel 
  • Old rice mill Hub Ho Hin Museum 
  • Khao Tang Kuan (Chinese Kuan Uu shrine)  
  • Two Seas Public Library (Song Le Library) 
  • Misiem Yipintsoi Open Museum
