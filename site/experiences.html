On the hill

Walk, cook, practice, gather.

Programs are arranged for guests and for groups. Write ahead so the kitchen and the guides can make room for you.

Forest

Hikes

Trails from half an hour to three hours, along the forest paths and down by the stream. The long hike to the rock pool leaves after breakfast and returns for lunch.

  • 200 baht per person for the rock-pool hike
  • 10 people minimum when the hike is booked as a group
  • Bring shoes you can walk in, and something to swim in

Kitchen

Thai cooking

A half-day class built around the dishes served at the resort. Five recipes, including a dessert, taught from the garden and the local market.

  • 13:30 – 17:00
  • 800 baht
  • English translation for foreign groups
  • Private groups from 10 people

Practice

Yoga and Chi Gong

Body-work sessions sit inside the resort’s health programs, and can be arranged privately for a group. Ask by email for the next open session.

Health

Love Your Health

A short detox stay with simple meals, qigong, and meditation. Programs run about three or four days for private guests and groups. Current rates are confirmed when you write.

Groups

Retreats and seminars

A meeting hall with audio-visual equipment, forest rooms, and vegetarian cooking. Teams use the hill for their own workshops, then add a few days on the islands of Satun if they wish.

Beyond the hill

Island days

Koh Tarutao, Koh Lipe, Koh Adang, and Koh Rawi are a few hours south. December to March is the busy beach season, so book boats ahead. We can help sketch a family, adventure, or culture itinerary.

Volunteering

From time to time the resort looks for people with a specific skill: kitchen, garden, teaching, or care of the grounds. Write with what you can offer and how long you can stay. It is not an open work-exchange.
